Legacy & Vision

About RVITM

Shaping the future of Science and Technology through experiential learning, academic rigour, and societal commitment.

80+ Years of Academic Excellence

Established in 2019 under the banner of **RV Educational Institutions (RVEI)** and managed by **Rashtreeya Sikshana Samithi Trust (RSST)**, RVITM is dedicated to building the innovators of tomorrow.

Founded in 1940 by the visionary freedom fighter Sri M.C. Sivananda Sarma, RVEI began as a single classroom with just six students. Today, it has blossomed into a mighty ecosystem of over 27 premier institutions educating 24,000+ students. Rooted in Gandhian values yet driven by 21st-century innovation, RVITM offers an urban, high-tech engineering education in South Bengaluru.

🎯 Vision

To impart top-quality technical education with global competence and societal commitment, leading to breakthrough innovations.

🌱 Mission

To nurture passionate engineers through experiential learning by providing state-of-the-art infrastructure and ICT-enabled education.

Admission Gateway

Admissions 2026

Transparent, merit-based entry pathways to join Bengaluru’s top technical institution.

Karnataka Domicile

KCET (KEA Quota)

Secured via state counselling conducted by Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA). Open to Karnataka candidates who qualify in the CET entrance examination.

All India Merit

COMED-K Quota

Open to students from across India through the COMED-K entrance examination and centralized online counselling allocation.

Institutional Merit

Management Quota

Direct institutional seats allocated based on 10+2 marks & entrance performance, administered centrally by the Rashtreeya Sikshana Samithi Trust (RSST).
