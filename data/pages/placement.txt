## PLACEMENT OVERVIEW
The Training and Placement Cell at RVITM works closely with industry to help students transition from academics to professional careers. The institute benefits from a combined placement ecosystem with RVCE, enabling students to access a larger pool of recruiters and career opportunities.

## Frequently Asked Questions
•
What is the placement support provided by RVITM?
The institute offers training programs, internships, career guidance, and campus recruitment opportunities through the placement cell.
Do students get internship opportunities?
Yes, students are encouraged to undertake internships with technology companies, startups, and engineering firms.
Which companies recruit from RVITM?
Companies from the software, electronics, consulting, and emerging technology sectors participate in campus recruitment.
How does RVITM prepare students for placements?
Students receive training in technical skills, aptitude, communication, resume building, and interview preparation.
