## Frequently Asked Questions
What facilities are available at RVITM?
The campus includes modern classrooms, laboratories, a library, sports facilities, a cafeteria and student activity spaces.
Are sports facilities available for students?
Yes. Students can participate in a variety of indoor and outdoor sports activities.
Does RVITM provide library and research resources?
Yes. The library offers textbooks, journals, digital resources, and academic databases.
Are there spaces for student collaboration?
Yes. The campus provides student common areas and activity spaces for discussions, projects, and club activities.
