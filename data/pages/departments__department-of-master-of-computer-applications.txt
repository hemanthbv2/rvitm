The Department of Master of Computer Applications (MCA) at RVITM focuses on developing highly skilled software professionals equipped with advanced computing knowledge and practical programming expertise.
The programme prepares students for careers in the rapidly evolving software industry by providing strong foundations in:
## Programming and software development
## Data analytics and information systems
## Cloud computing and distributed systems

## Frequently Asked Questions
•
What is the duration of the MCA programme at RVITM?
The Master of Computer Applications (MCA) programme at RVITM is a 2-year postgraduate programme designed to develop advanced skills in software development, data technologies and computing systems.
Is the MCA programme at RVITM affiliated and approved?
Yes. The MCA programme at RVITM is:
• Approved by All India Council for Technical Education
• Affiliated to Visvesvaraya Technological University
• This ensures the programme meets national standards for technical education.
What are the eligibility criteria for MCA admission?
To apply for the MCA programme, candidates must:
• Hold a Bachelor’s degree from a recognised university
• Have studied Mathematics / Statistics / Computer Science / Computer Programming at the undergraduate or 10+2 level
Meet the eligibility criteria prescribed by VTU and admission authorities.
•
What entrance exams are accepted for MCA admission?
Admission to the MCA programme may be available through:
• Karnataka PGCET
• Institutional admission process (management quota)
Applicants should refer to the official admission guidelines for detailed information.
What subjects are covered in the MCA curriculum?
The MCA curriculum includes advanced computing subjects such as:
• Data Structures and Algorithms
• Database Management Systems
• Software Engineering
• Artificial Intelligence
• Cloud Computing
• Data Analytics
The programme combines theoretical learning with practical laboratory sessions and projects .
•
What makes RVITM a good choice for MCA?
RVITM offers several advantages for MCA students:
• Industry-oriented curriculum
• Experienced faculty and modern laboratories
• Strong software development training
• Access to Bengaluru’s technology ecosystem
These factors help students build successful careers in the IT industry.
Are internships part of the MCA programme?
Yes. Students are encouraged to participate in internships and industry projects to gain practical experience and exposure to real-world software development environments.
What industries hire MCA graduates?
MCA graduates are recruited by companies in sectors such as:
• Information Technology
• Software Product Development
• Data Analytics
• FinTech
• Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
