## Dr. M.P. Shyam
## President, RVEI
At RV Institute of Technology and Management (RVITM), education is viewed as a transformative experience that prepares young minds to engage confidently with a changing technological landscape. As a constituent institution of RV Educational Institutions (RVEI), RVITM reflects a long-standing commitment to academic excellence shaped by strong values and social responsibility.
The institution is dedicated to developing engineers who demonstrate technical competence alongside integrity. Learning at RVITM connects theory with application through meaningful academic engagement and exposure to industry practices. Faculty mentorship and a supportive academic environment encourage students to question, explore ideas and apply knowledge with clarity of purpose.
RVITM continues to evolve as a centre of progressive education where ambition finds direction through opportunity. Each student's journey is recognised as distinct and supported with thoughtful guidance. Through consistent academic rigour and a future-focused outlook, the institution seeks to nurture professionals prepared to contribute responsibly to technology and society.
