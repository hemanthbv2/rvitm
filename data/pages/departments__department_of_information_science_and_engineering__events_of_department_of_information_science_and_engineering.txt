The Toyota Hackathon 2024, organised by Toyota Kirloskar Motors, unfolded on February 3rd and 4th, 2024, at RVITM. Aimed at students in grades 9 to 12, the event saw enthusiastic participation from schools across Bangalore.
The hackathon focused on key themes crucial to road safety and community engagement. Participants delved into topics such as School Zone Safety, Road Safety Awareness and Education, Community Engagement for Safety, IOT/ICT in Road Safety, Inclusivity on Roads, and Policy Improvement under the Motor Vehicles Act 1988.
Coordinator: Dr. Niharika P. Kumar
