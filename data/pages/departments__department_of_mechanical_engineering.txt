## ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT
The Department of Mechanical Engineering at RVITM, Bengaluru, focuses on developing engineers with strong fundamentals, practical skills and the ability to innovate in modern engineering environments.
The programme offers comprehensive education in core mechanical disciplines, including:
## Thermodynamics
## Fluid Mechanics
## Machine Design
## Manufacturing Processes
## Materials Engineering
## Dynamics and Control Systems

## MESSAGE FROM HoD
## Dr. C. Solaimuthu
## Head of Mechanical Engineering, RVITM
The Department of Mechanical Engineering at RV Institute of Technology and Management (RVITM), Bengaluru, has been a cornerstone of excellence in Engineering Education and Research since its inception in 2019. With a strong foundation in fundamental principles and a forward-looking approach towards emerging technologies, the department continues to nurture competent, innovative, and socially responsible engineers.
Our teaching is designed to blend theoretical knowledge with practical exposure through industry collaborations, internships, and multi-disciplinary projects as per the VTU Curriculum and Syllabus. The department’s highly qualified, experienced, committed and dedicated faculty members guide students in areas such as Thermal, Design, and Manufacturing Engineering. The department has experienced technical Staff to support them.
We take pride in our alumni who have excelled in diverse fields across the country and continue to contribute to technological advancement and societal development.
With a commitment to continuous improvement and innovation, the Department of Mechanical Engineering at RVITM strives to be a leader in education, research, and industrial engagement, empowering our students to engineer a better future.
