## Frequently Asked Questions
•
Who can join the RVITM Alumni Network?
All graduates of RVITM are eligible to join the alumni network.
What are the benefits of joining the alumni portal?
The portal enables networking, mentorship opportunities, event updates, and career connections.
Can alumni contribute to student development?
Yes. Alumni regularly mentor students, conduct industry talks, and assist with internships and career guidance.
Does RVITM organise alumni events?
Yes, the institute hosts alumni meets, networking sessions, and engagement programs throughout the year.
