## Frequently Asked Questions
•
What programmes are included in the CSE Cluster at RVITM?
The CSE Cluster includes Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), CSE – Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, and Information Science and Engineering (ISE).
What career opportunities are available after studying in the CSE Cluster?
Students can pursue careers in software development, AI/ML, cloud computing, data analytics, cybersecurity, and emerging digital technologies.
Are the programmes affiliated with VTU?
Yes. All programmes are affiliated with VTU and approved by AICTE.
Does RVITM provide placement support for computing programmes?
Yes. Students receive placement training, technical preparation, internship opportunities, and career guidance through the Training and Placement Cell.
