## ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT
The Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at RVITM, Bengaluru, is committed to developing industry-ready engineers equipped with expertise in electronics design, communication systems, embedded technologies, VLSI, IoT and next-generation digital systems.
Located in South Bengaluru, RVITM offers a strong academic ecosystem, backed by modern laboratories, experienced faculty, and a structured curriculum, and is affiliated with Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU) and approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE).

## Vision & Mission
VISION
To foster a globally competitive environment in Electronics and Communication Engineering through cutting-edge technological skills, innovation and interdisciplinary research to address societal needs.
Mission
• To provide an innovative teaching learning environment through ICT enabled infrastructure that enhances knowledge acquisition in significant and sustainable ways.
• To inculcate problem-solving skills, interdisciplinary research capabilities, entrepreneurial mindset and teamwork among students, while upholding strong ethical and moral values, to develop niche expertise and achieve professional excellence.
• To emphasize the holistic development of students through value-added skill enhancement programs that bridge the industry-academia gap, address societal needs, and encourage the pursuit of higher education.
## Message from HoD
## Dr. Manjunatha Prasad R
## Vice Principal, Professor & Head,
Department of Electronics & Communication Engineering, RVITM
With an intake of 60 students, the department is committed to educating and empowering future engineers to create a smarter and more sustainable world. The department is equipped with the requisite infrastructure, modern laboratories, advanced computing facilities, Smart classrooms and high-speed internet access, which collectively provide an excellent learning environment.
Electronics and Communication Engineering is a dynamic field that powers modern technologies—from Wireless communication and Embedded Systems to VLSI design, IoT, Robotics and Artificial intelligence.
Read More
Our department strives to provide a strong foundation in core areas such as Analogue and Digital Electronics, Signal Processing, Image & Speech Processing. IOT & Microcontrollers, Communication Systems and VLSI Technology. With well-equipped laboratories, experienced faculty members, and a research-oriented environment, we ensure that our students gain both theoretical knowledge and practical expertise.
We encourage students to participate in conferences, technical workshops, hackathons, internships, research projects, industry collaborations, and professional bodies to enhance their skills and employability. Our goal is to nurture innovative thinkers, responsible engineers, and ethical professionals who can contribute meaningfully to society and technological advancement.
The students are successfully placed in both core electronics companies and leading software firms. The department remains committed to addressing societal challenges through innovation and technological advancement.
The future of ECE is boundless, with its applications driving advancements in diverse sectors such as healthcare, telecommunications, space exploration and renewable energy. As part of this vibrant and dynamic domain, you will not only gain technical prowess but also contribute meaningfully to society, creating technologies that transform lives.
