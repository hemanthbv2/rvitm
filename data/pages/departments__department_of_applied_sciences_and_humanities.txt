## ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT
The Department of Applied Sciences and Humanities at RVITM plays a vital role in building the academic foundation for undergraduate engineering students.
The department focuses on strengthening the scientific, analytical and communication skills that underpin engineering education.
It integrates key disciplines, including:
## Mathematics
## Physics
## Chemistry
## Communication and Humanities
Through a blend of theoretical knowledge, laboratory experiments, and interdisciplinary learning, the department prepares students for advanced engineering studies across various technical domains.
## VISION AND MISSION
Vision
•
To nurture budding engineers with strong foundations in Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry, enabling them to understand and leverage modern technologies, engage in multidisciplinary learning, and contribute to innovation, research, and the development of inclusive and sustainable technologies.
Mission
•
To build a strong conceptual foundation in Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and Humanities essential for engineering education.
•
To develop analytical thinking, scientific temper, problem-solving ability, and effective communication skills.
•
To create an interdisciplinary learning environment that fosters innovation, research, and employability.
•
To promote ethical values, lifelong learning, and socially responsible leadership among students.
## CORE DISCIPLINES
The department includes specialised academic groups that contribute to the foundational curriculum.
Mathematics
The Department of Mathematics at RVTM focuses on solving socio-economic, technological, and work-related challenges. It aims to provide a strong foundation in mathematics for advanced studies, fostering excellence in education and research, while promoting student involvement in seminars and discussions.
Engineering mathematics equips students with analytical tools used in:
• Data science
• Artificial intelligence
• Electronics and signal processing
• Control systems
Physics
Engineering Physics is a foundational subject for all first-year engineering students at Visvesvaraya Technological University. The department has excellent infrastructure and spacious, well-equipped laboratories. Faculty members are qualified and experienced, actively engaging in both teaching and interdisciplinary research.
Physics forms the scientific basis for:
• Electronics and semiconductor technologies
• Material science
• Optical communication
• Nanotechnology
Chemistry
The Department of Chemistry, established in 2019, offers Engineering Chemistry courses to first-year engineering students, providing essential knowledge and practical skills. Our experienced faculty conduct research in environmental science, biosensors, electroanalysis, and nanomaterials, focusing on the impact of chemistry on energy, the environment, and human health. Engineering chemistry supports advancements in:
• Material engineering
• Nanomaterials
• Energy storage technologies
• Environmental engineering
Humanities & Communication
Humanities courses help students develop:
• Professional communication
• Ethics in engineering
• Critical thinking
• Leadership and teamwork skills

## Frequently Asked Questions
•
Why are Applied Sciences important in engineering?
Applied sciences provide the scientific and mathematical foundation required to understand complex engineering concepts.
What subjects are taught in the Applied Sciences department?
The department covers mathematics, physics, chemistry, communication skills and interdisciplinary sciences.
How does this department help engineering students?
It strengthens analytical thinking, scientific understanding, and communication skills, all of which are essential to engineering education.
Are laboratory experiments part of the curriculum?
Yes. Students conduct experiments in physics and chemistry laboratories to gain practical scientific understanding.
