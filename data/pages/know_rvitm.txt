## Our Founding Institution
Founded in 1940 by the visionary freedom fighter Sri M.C. Sivananda Sarma, RVEI began as a single classroom with just six students. Today, managed by the RV Educational Institutions (RVEI), it has blossomed into a mighty tree of over 27 premier institutions.
With a rich 80+ year legacy, RVEI is a household name synonymous with academic rigour and societal commitment. From kindergarten to doctoral research, the group nurtures over 24,000+ students. Rooted in Gandhian values yet driven by 21st-century innovation, RVEI continues to define the educational landscape of Bengaluru, proving that true excellence is a journey, not a destination.
