## ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT
The Department of Physical Education at RVITM focuses on promoting physical fitness, sports participation, and overall well-being among students. The department believes that physical education is an essential component of holistic education, helping students develop discipline, leadership, teamwork and resilience. Students are encouraged to actively participate in sports alongside their academic pursuits, fostering a balanced, healthy campus life. The department organises sports events, training programmes, and competitions to nurture athletic talent and encourage a culture of active living within the institute.
## MISSION
The Department of Physical Education aims to:
• Strengthening of existing facilities
• Promoting fitness to students and staff
• Attracting mass participation
• Creating new facilities
• Proper maintenance of all playgrounds
• To improve the spirit of sportsmanship

## Frequently Asked Questions
Why is physical education important for engineering students?
Physical education promotes fitness, mental well-being, teamwork, and leadership skills that complement academic learning.
What sports facilities are available at RVITM?
The institute provides facilities for outdoor and indoor sports, including cricket, football, badminton, and table tennis.
Are students encouraged to participate in sports competitions?
Yes. Students regularly participate in inter-department and inter-college sports events.
Does RVITM organise sports events?
Yes. The Department of Physical Education organises annual sports meets, tournaments, and recreational activities.
