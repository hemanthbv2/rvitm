Government of Karnataka

This No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the Government of Karnataka (GOK) is a vital regulatory milestone for RVITM. It officially authorises the institute to restructure its course offerings and intake. This approval underscores our agility in aligning with current educational demands while maintaining full compliance with state and university standards.

VTU

The VTU Affiliation Notification is a critical credential granting RVITM the legal authority to conduct its academic programs. It confirms the institute meets rigorous university standards for curriculum and infrastructure. This certification ensures all degrees awarded are recognised, valid and aligned with state-level technical requirements.
