ADMISSION PATHWAYS

Admissions to the undergraduate (B.E.) programmes at RVITM are available through the following routes:

KCET

Karnataka Students

Students from Karnataka can secure admission through the state entrance examination conducted by the Karnataka Examination Authority.

COMED-K

All India

Students from across India can apply through the COMED-K entrance examination.

Management Quota

Institutional

Limited seats are available under institutional admission for eligible candidates.

Admissions to the postgraduate (MCA) programmes at RVITM are available through the following routes:

PGCET

Karnataka Students

Students from Karnataka can secure admission through the state entrance examination conducted by the Karnataka Examination Authority.

Management Quota

Institutional

Limited seats are available under institutional admission for eligible candidates.

Must be eligible with completion of a 3-year UG programme like B.Sc. / BCA.

ELIGIBILITY

CRITERIA

Undergraduate Engineering Programmes

10+2 or equivalent examination

Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects

Chemistry / Biotechnology / Computer Science / Electronics as optional subjects

Candidates must also qualify in relevant entrance examinations such as KCET or COMED-K.

Postgraduate Programmes

Eligibility criteria depend on the programme and relevant entrance requirements.

ADMISSION PROCESS

The admission process typically involves the following steps:

Step 01
Appear for the relevant entrance examination (KCET, COMED-K or PGCET).
Step 02
Participate in counselling conducted by the respective authority.
Step 03
Select RVITM as the preferred institution during counselling.
Step 04
Complete document verification and seat confirmation.
Step 05
Finalise admission by submitting required documents and fees.

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR UG

Applicants are typically required to submit the following documents:

10th standard marks card

12th standard marks card

Entrance exam rank card (KCET / COMED-K / PGCET)

Transfer certificate

Migration certificate (if applicable)

Identity proof

Passport-size photographs

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR PG

Applicants are typically required to submit the following documents:

10th standard marks card

12th standard marks card

Graduation Marksheets(BCA or B.Sc.)

Provisional / Degree Certificate

Entrance exam rank card (KCET / COMED-K / PGCET)

Transfer certificate

Migration certificate (if applicable)

Identity proof

Passport-size photographs

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I apply for admission to RVITM?

Students can apply through KCET, COMED-K, or the management quota, depending on eligibility.

Is RVITM affiliated with VTU?

Yes, the institute is affiliated with VTU and approved by AICTE.

What engineering programmes are available at RVITM?

Programmes include Computer Science, AI & Machine Learning, Information Science, Electronics & Communication, and Mechanical Engineering.

Does RVITM provide placement support?

Yes, the institute offers placement training, internships, and recruitment opportunities through the placement cell.
