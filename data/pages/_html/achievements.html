INSTITUTIONAL ACHIEVEMENTS

  • 2024

Sambhram 2026

Sambhram 2026, RVITM's inter college cultural extravaganza, was celebrated on 23rd and 24th April 2026. The festivities for Sambhram 2026, started with a pre event. The stage for the main was adorned by Actor and Big Boss Kannada 11 star, Mr. Dharma Keerthiraj, This year’s edition, themed “Roots and Rhythms”, celebrated the evolution of art and cultural expression across the ages.300 plus registrations spanning across several colleges and events was a record at RVITM. The festival featured over 26 diverse events, including performing arts, literary competitions, fine arts and digital showcases—providing a vibrant platform for students to exhibit their talents and engage with peers from institutions across the region. Our annual cultural Newsletter Kalaamayaa, showcasing the happenings of the Cultural Club, was also released on this occasion. It also showcased the gorgeous talent of our own RVITM students, by way of their performances for the Inauguration event. The event also saw the grand concert performance by SWAR
