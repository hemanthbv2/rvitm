## SERB funded one day workshop on Advanced Materials - Characterization and Electrochemical Applications
Date: 29/01/2024
Faculty Coordinators:
Samrat D
The workshop focused on the introduction to the design and development of new hybrid and functional nanoscale and quantum materials aimed at cleaner and more sustainable energy alternatives. Detailed sessions were organized on the characterization of advanced materials, providing participants with comprehensive insights into modern analytical techniques. Fundamental aspects of key electrochemical methods were also discussed to enhance understanding of the applications of advanced materials in sensing technologies and energy systems.
## SERB funded one day workshop on Advanced Materials Synthesis: Applications for Energy Generation and Storage
Date: 13/09/2024
Faculty Coordinators:
B E Prasad
The workshop focused on the design, synthesis, and characterization of functional nanoscale and quantum materials for clean and sustainable energy applications. It also highlighted fundamental principles and recent advances in energy generation, battery science, and emerging energy technologies.

## Technical Aptitude Test
Event Coordinator: Dr. Gangadharaiah Y H
Date: 08/06/2023
Faculty Coordinators:
Dr. Sivasankar S
Dr. Nagabhushana P
Dr. Sushma M P
A technical aptitude test for RVITM students was organised by department of mathematics in association with NDLI club, RVITM. The test was conducted for a duration of 60 minutes. The main content of test included question related to logical reasoning, quantitative and qualitative analysis and other mathematics related concepts. These questions were framed in view point of aptitude round of placement training process. The test enables students to develop basic skills to prepare for aptitude test round of placement training process. Students showed enthusiastic participation in writing test.

## The Technical Talk on “Predictive Data Analytics -a Mathematical Perspective”
Event Coordinator: Dr. K Sangeeta, Data Science Trainer/Educator, Bengaluru
Date: 13-03-2021
Chief Patron: Dr. Jayapal R
Convener: Dr. Gangadharaiah Y H
Session Coordinators: Dr. Smitha T V, Dr. Nagabhushana P
The Technical Talk on “Predictive Data Analytics -a Mathematical Perspective” began at 12:20 PM by welcoming the gathering and introduction to the resource person by Dr. Smitha T V. An Invocation song was recited by Nitya Ananth, 1st Sem, CSE Dept. Student. Thereafter, Principal Dr. Jayapal R gave the presidential address. Then, followed by a fascinating session by the resource person on “Predictive Data Analytics -a Mathematical Perspective”. She had discussed about predictive models, analysis of data, designing of model by taking simple data and mathematics involved in all the analysis. She had explained all these concepts very well with practical applications. It was overall an interesting, informative and interactive session. Students interacted well during the session. Her session went on till 01:50 PM. At 01:55 PM, the function was concluded by vote of thanks by Dr. Nagabhushana P.
