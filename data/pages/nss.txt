## ABOUT NSS
The National Service Scheme (NSS) is a government-sponsored programme that aims to develop students' personalities and character through voluntary community service.
At RVITM , the NSS unit provides students with opportunities to engage in meaningful social initiatives that benefit society while fostering leadership, teamwork, and civic responsibility.
Through various outreach programmes, students gain a deeper understanding of social challenges and learn to contribute positively to their communities.
## Motto
The Motto of NSS, “Not Me But You”, reflects the essence of democratic living and upholds the need for selfless service. NSS helps students develop an appreciation for another person’s point of view and shows consideration for other living beings.
The philosophy of the NSS is well documented in this motto, which underlines the belief that the welfare of an individual is ultimately dependent on the welfare of society as a whole; therefore, NSS volunteers shall strive for the well-being of society.
## OBJECTIVES OF NSS
The NSS programme focuses on developing socially responsible citizens through active community participation.
## Key Objectives
•
Understand the community in which we work
•
Understand ourselves in relation to our community
•
Identify the needs and problems of the community and involve ourselves in problem-solving
•
Develop among ourselves a sense of social and civic responsibility
•
Utilise their knowledge in finding practical solutions to individual and community problems
•
Develop the competence required for group living and sharing of responsibilities
•
Gain skills in mobilising community participation
•
Acquire leadership qualities and democratic attitudes
•
Develop the capacity to meet emergencies and natural disasters
•
Practice national integration and social harmony

## Frequently Asked Questions
•
What is the National Service Scheme (NSS)?
The National Service Scheme is a student volunteer programme that encourages participation in community service and social development activities.
Who can join the NSS unit at RVITM?
Students from all departments at RVITM are encouraged to join the NSS unit and participate in its activities.
What are the benefits of participating in NSS?
NSS participation helps students develop leadership, teamwork, social awareness, and community engagement skills.
Do students receive recognition for NSS participation?
Yes. Students receive certificates and recognition for active participation in NSS activities and programmes.
