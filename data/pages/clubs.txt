JOINING STUDENT CLUBS
Participation in student clubs helps students develop several valuable skills.

Students learn to plan events, lead teams, and manage projects.

## Frequently Asked Questions
•
What are student clubs at RVITM?
Student clubs are student-led groups that focus on technical, cultural, and social activities.
Can students from any department join clubs?
Yes. Most clubs are open to students from all departments.
What are the benefits of joining a club?
Joining a club helps students develop leadership skills, technical knowledge, and teamwork experience.
Do clubs organise events and competitions?
Yes. Clubs frequently organize workshops, competitions, hackathons, and cultural events.
