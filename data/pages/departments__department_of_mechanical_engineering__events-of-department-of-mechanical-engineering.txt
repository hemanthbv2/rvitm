## RVITM Premier League (RPL) 2026
The RPL 2026 Auction was held on 13.12.2025 , marking the official kickoff of the tournament. The RPL 2026 Season Opener and Matches were conducted on 01.01.2026 at the RVITM campus. The event was graced by the presence of Dr. Nagashettappa Biradar, Principal , and Dr. Manjunatha Prasad R. Vice Principal , RVITM.
The tournament concluded with Hoysala Strickers emerging as Winners and RV Super Kings as Runners-up .
Man of the Series: Dr. Durga Prasad C
Best Bowler: Mrs. Nandini T K
Best Batsman: Mr. Sachin K M
The event was successfully organized by the RPL 2026 Organizing Committee comprising Dr. Gajanan M. Naik, Dr. Lokesh P, Dr. Manish Kumar, Dr. Niharika P. Kumar, and Dr. Shalini J .
