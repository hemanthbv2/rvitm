ABOUT THE PROGRAMME
## B.E. in Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)
Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) is a vital and dynamic field focused on the design and development of electronic and communication systems. The curriculum covers subjects like circuit analysis, digital electronics, communication systems, signal processing, embedded systems, microcontrollers and programming. ECE is rapidly evolving and is crucial for advancing modern technology.
## Core Areas Covered:
Analogue & Digital Electronics
Signals & Systems
Microprocessors & Microcontrollers
VLSI Design
Communication Systems
Control Systems
Embedded Systems
Wireless Communication

## Programme Educational Objectives(PEOs)
## PEO1
Apply fundamental knowledge of Electronics, Integrated Circuit Concepts,
Communication Systems, Signal Processing, and Embedded Systems to solve real-
world engineering problems and pursue advanced studies
## PEO2
Demonstrate the use of modern tools and multidisciplinary techniques for
designing, comprehending and developing innovative solutions to real world
problems.
## PEO3
Exhibit entrepreneurial skills, uphold ethical practices, and engage in
lifelong learning for successful professional and societal growth.
## Programme Specific Outcomes(PSO)
## PSO1
Apply electronic design and integrated circuit (IC) concepts for the analysis and synthesis of analog and digital circuit configurations.
## PSO2
Demonstrate technical competency in signal processing and communication systems, with exposure to Embedded Systems.
## PSO3
Possess application level knowledge in interdisciplinary domains.
