## Dr.(h.c.) A.V.S. Murthy
## Hon. Secretary, RVEI
As a premier destination within the RV Educational Institutions (RVEI) family, RV Institute of Technology and Management (RVITM) carries forward a storied 80-year legacy of academic brilliance. While the roots of this organisation are deep, the vision remains firmly fixed on the horizon. RVITM exists to be more than just an engineering college; it is a specialised, ICT-enabled ecosystem built for the next generation of digital pioneers.
At RVITM, the focus remains on bridging the gap between traditional theory and the fast-paced demands of the global tech industry. By leveraging a state-of-the-art facility and a unique synergy with the wider RV network, students gain unparalleled exposure, modern pedagogical tools and a direct pathway to professional success.
The mission here is to cultivate the leadership and critical thinking required to master it. Whether as a student, a parent, or an industry partner, everyone is invited to witness a redefinition of technical education. The future is being engineered right here.
