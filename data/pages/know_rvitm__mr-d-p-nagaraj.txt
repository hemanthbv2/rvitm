## Mr. D.P. Nagaraj
## Hon. Joint Secretary, RVEI
RV Institute of Technology and Management (RVITM) is a forward-looking institution driven by a clear mission: to connect academic learning with the realities of modern industry. Built on a legacy of excellence, the institute continues to shape its own identity through purposeful innovation.
The curriculum emphasises emerging disciplines such as Computer Science, Information Science, Electronics and Mechanical Engineering. Students graduate prepared to contribute meaningfully in professional environments. Advanced laboratories and experienced faculty create an atmosphere that encourages curiosity and independent growth.
Every student is seen as a future contributor to society, capable of shaping what comes next. Parents and industry partners can expect a steadfast commitment to integrity and consistent academic standards.
Change defines the present world, and RVITM responds with confidence and direction. It sets its course with intent rather than imitation. This is an invitation to be part of a journey shaped by progress and purpose.
