## ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT
The Department of Information Science and Engineering (ISE) at RVITM focuses on building strong foundations in information systems, software engineering, data technologies, and distributed computing.
Information Science is a key discipline within computer engineering that focuses on data management, software systems, networking technologies, and modern computing infrastructure .
The department aims to develop engineers who can design scalable digital systems, manage complex data environments, and develop innovative software solutions for modern industries.

## Vision & Mission
VISION
To mould the students into Professional Engineers through Research and Development, to meet the societal and future challenges in the domain of Information Science and Engineering.
Mission
• To nurture students with quality education in Information Science and Engineering by providing strong theoretical and practical background with emphasis on ICT enabled teaching.
• To create an open environment amongst students, faculty, alumni and Industry to facilitate exchange of ideas and promote an atmosphere where research, innovation and entrepreneurship can flourish.
• To inculcate discipline, human values, professional ethics and promote team work through interdisciplinary projects, co-curricular and societal activities

## Frequently Asked Questions
•
What is Information Science Engineering?
Information Science focuses on designing and managing software systems, data technologies, and information infrastructures.
What career opportunities are available after ISE?
Graduates can work in software development, cloud computing, data engineering, and information technology.
Is the program affiliated to VTU?
Yes. The program follows the VTU curriculum and is approved by AICTE.
Does RVITM provide placement assistance?
Yes. Students receive placement training, internships, and recruitment opportunities through the placement cell.
