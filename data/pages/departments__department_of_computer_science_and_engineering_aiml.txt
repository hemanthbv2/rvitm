## MESSAGE FROM HoD
## Dr. Surbhi Agrawal
## HoD, CSE (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning)
Welcome to the Department of Computer Science and Engineering (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning). Artificial Intelligence is transforming every sector—from healthcare and education to industry and governance—and our department is committed to preparing students to be leaders in this rapidly evolving technological landscape.
Our vision is to build a strong foundation in core computer science while equipping students with advanced knowledge in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, and emerging technologies. We emphasise experiential learning through hands-on laboratories, industry collaborations, research projects, hackathons, and innovation initiatives, enabling students to solve real-world problems using intelligent systems.
The department fosters a culture of research, creativity, and continuous learning. Our dedicated faculty members actively engage in research, publications, and industry collaborations to ensure students receive a relevant, future-ready education. We also encourage students to develop interdisciplinary skills, ethical responsibility, and an innovative mindset aligned with the vision of Atmanirbhar Bharat.
As a newly established department, we are committed to creating an ecosystem that nurtures academic excellence, innovation, and professional growth. We look forward to shaping competent engineers, researchers, and innovators who will contribute meaningfully to society and the global AI community.

## Frequently Asked Questions
•
Is AI and ML a good career option?
Yes. Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning are among the fastest-growing technology domains globally.
What jobs can I get after AI and ML engineering?
Graduates can work as AI engineers, data scientists, machine learning engineers and software developers.
Is the AI and ML program affiliated with VTU?
Yes, the program follows the VTU curriculum and is approved by AICTE.
Does RVITM provide placement assistance?
Yes, students receive placement training, internships, and career guidance through the placement cell.
