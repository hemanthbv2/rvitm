## Programme Educational Objectives(PEOs)
## PEO1
Graduates will demonstrate advanced knowledge and proficiency in computer applications, showcasing their ability to analyze complex problems and apply innovative solutions effectively in diverse technological contexts.
## PEO2
Graduates will engage in research and development activities, contributing to advancements in computer applications and making contributions to industry practices and technological solutions.
## PEO3
Graduates will exhibit ethical leadership, emphasizing social responsibility in their application of computer technologies. They will demonstrate a global perspective, considering cultural and societal implications in their decision-making.
## PEO4
Graduates will excel in communication skills, both written and verbal, facilitating clear conveyance of technical information. They will commit to lifelong learning, adapting to emerging technologies and acquiring new skills throughout their career.
