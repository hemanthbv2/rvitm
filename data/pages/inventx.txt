Turn Ideas into Innovation with InventX
At RVITM, innovation goes beyond classrooms. InventX is a collaborative platform that brings together students, faculty, alumni, mentors, researchers, investors, and industry professionals to foster entrepreneurship, technology innovation and interdisciplinary collaboration.
Whether you are building a project, exploring startup ideas, or looking to connect with innovators, inventX provides the ecosystem to transform ideas into impactful solutions.
## What students can do through InventX
## Showcase innovative projects and ideas
## Connect with mentors, alumni, and industry experts
## Explore startup and entrepreneurship opportunities
## Participate in innovation-driven communities and events
## Collaborate across technology and research domains
## Why InventX Matters
The platform empowers students to move from learning concepts to building real-world solutions .
InventX encourages a culture of:
## Problem-solving and experimentation
## Industry interaction and networking
## Startup thinking and entrepreneurship
## Innovation and creativity
Innovation Begins Here
From engineering projects and research ideas to startup collaborations and mentorship opportunities, inventX helps students gain exposure to the innovation ecosystem in Bengaluru , India’s leading technology and startup hub.
